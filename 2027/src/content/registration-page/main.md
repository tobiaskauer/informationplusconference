We are currently defining the registration fees and conditions for Information+ 2027.

This year we are working on a **new registration model** designed to broaden participation, with particular attention to making the conference more accessible to participants from the **Global South**.

Details on fees, deadlines, and how to register will be published on this page as soon as they are confirmed. For any questions, write to [general@infoplus.team](mailto:general@infoplus.team).
